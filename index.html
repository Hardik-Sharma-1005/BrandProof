Privacy and data

Review drafts, decisions, sources and history are kept in this browser's local storage. During a scan, the browser sends the draft and enabled source excerpts to the local BrandProof service for matching. The service returns findings and does not save them. Uploaded files are parsed in memory.

This demo does not send content to an AI provider or other external service. Use the clear-data control to remove saved demo records from this browser. The role selector is for demonstration and does not provide account security.

Terms of use

BrandProof is a fictional prototype for demonstrating a content review workflow. Its rules and suggested edits are illustrative and are not legal, clinical or regulatory advice.

A clean scan is not a compliance guarantee. A qualified human reviewer should assess the final copy and its supporting evidence before publication. Do not use this local demo for confidential production material.
